01 / About

Design engineer, production first.

I am a mechanical engineering student at the University of Texas at Austin's Cockrell School, graduating in December 2027 with a minor in computer science. Two internships at SpaceX put me on the factory floor at Starbase — first designing weld cells, lift tooling, and flight-part retrofits for Starship, then serving as Responsible Engineer for the 13,000+ component assembly that seals the Booster's 33 engine feedlines during Cryo Proof.

The through-line in my work is designing hardware that survives contact with the people who have to build it. That means GD&T a machinist can actually hold, tooling that is ergonomic on a real production line, and adjustment mechanisms a trackside engineer can service in seconds. I would rather disposition an issue standing next to the part than argue about it in a review.

On Longhorn Racing Electric I have worked every corner of vehicle dynamics — suspension, unsprung, brakes — and now lecture new members on the structural fundamentals behind it.
